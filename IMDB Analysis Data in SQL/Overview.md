**Main Questions** 

    Who Wins the Oscars?

Ever wondered who specifically wins the oscars, and even more specifically, how much money it would take to do so? Let's find out!
Using the Data sets at the bottom of this problem set, answer the following questions:

1. Who makes all the movies?
    a. Which studio makes the most movies?
    b. Which studio makes the most nominated movies?

2. What's the budget?

    a. Which films had a budget of over $300 million USD?
    b. Which of those films actually won the Oscars/Were nominated?

3. Which countries are most successful at winning the Oscars?

4. How much money does it take to win the Oscars? What are some of the flaws with this data?
    a. What was the average production cost per studio for all nominated movies? 
    Find the first five, with the highest first, and export as a csv!
    b. What was the average production cost per studio for all winning movies?
    Find the first five, with the highest first, and export as a csv!

5. Bonus: Using the data sets, find out another interesting fact about the winning Oscar movies!


We have three datasets to work with:

**Top 500 movie Data Set** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mitchellharrison/top-500-movies-budget

Top 500 movies by production budget, which are notably not inflation adjusted


**The Oscar Award Data Set** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/unanimad/the-oscar-award

This data set is a recording of past Academy Award winners and nominees between 1927 and 2025

The awards data was scraped from the Official Academy Awards search site; nominees were listed with their name first and film following in some categories, such as Best Actor/Actress, and in the reverse for others.


**Letterboxd** https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gsimonx37/letterboxd?resource=download

Movie data obtained from Letterboxd, updated anually.

Includes specific data about the movie, actors, crew, languages, csv, studios, countries, genres, themes, releases, and movie posters, which we did not include
